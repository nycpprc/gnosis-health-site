As I mentioned elsewhere on this site, around 2019, I began an autodidactic self-study. I have read and studied closely many books and listened to many audiobooks. The audiobooks I have listened to are listed on this page. To see the books I have studied, you may refer to this [link](/blog/books-i-am-reading).

When I listen to audiobooks, I do not usually pause or stop to take notes. Because of this, my comprehension is far lower on audiobooks compared to reading textually. Some audiobooks I can generally understand after a listen-through, but many others, which are more complex, I still cannot comprehend even after listening to them.

So whilst I have listened to all the items listed below, I fully admit that many of the complex ones I did not understand on listen through, for these, I will flag for deeper reading later.

I am sharing this so that people can understand what thought may have informed my own thinking, to inspire others to investigate the pieces listed here and to begin a dialogue. Please recommend pieces to check out or provide your interpretation or feedback on the items listed here.
